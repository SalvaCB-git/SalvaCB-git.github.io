    My final-year project. It collects public ransomware reports and uses a local language model with retrieval to identify MITRE ATT&CK techniques. I added a separate model to check the results and compared its judgements with human assessments. It received 10/10. The grade for the thesis. A sample of one.

    Krippendorff’s alpha 0.6461, N=278. One primary annotator, who is also the author.

    End-to-end F1 0.726 (95% CI 0.636–0.799, N=377).

    Python, Scrapy, Flask, SQLite, Docker, Ollama, ChromaDB

    Open the read-only demoRead the code

    Why two stages

    Mapping threat reports to ATT&CK is useful, but a language model’s output can’t be treated as ground truth. So extraction and validation are separate stages, and the pipeline keeps the evidence needed to measure where they agree and where they fail.

    What the first judge got wrong

    The first judge was Qwen re-judging its own output, and its most common error was vague abstraction: accepting a label such as T1486 (Data Encrypted for Impact) on a general mention of encryption, with no evidence that it was used for impact. That accounted for 187 of its 237 disagreements with the human annotator. The independent Gemma judge corrected 156 of the 161 it reviewed.

    187 errors of this type were recorded; 161 received a verdict from the second judge.

    Limits

    • Human calibration accepted 41.0% of confidence-1.0 candidates (N=100), and the Gemma judge accepted 41.27% (N=4,437). The samples are nested rather than independent, and the result does not establish equivalence within ±5 percentage points.

      Gemma judge: the independent second model. See How it runs.

    • True recall can’t be estimated from a candidate-only sample.
    • Human calibration used one primary annotator, who is also the author.
    • The stages use different sample sizes, so these metrics shouldn’t be compared as if they came from one test set.
    • Krippendorff’s alpha is 0.6461 on the stratified design sample (N=278). On the full production sample, weighted by the real mix of the corpus, it is 0.574 (N=377).
