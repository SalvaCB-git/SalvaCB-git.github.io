Bangkok

Country: Thailand

City or Region: Bangkok

Description: Bangkok is an exotic and vibrant city where tradition and modernity coexist in harmony. Buddhist temples like Wat Pho and Wat Arun stand out for their beauty and spirituality, while the Grand Palace dazzles with its royal architecture. Floating markets, bustling street life, and Thai massages make every day an adventure. Bangkok is also a food lover’s paradise, from street stalls serving pad thai to Michelin-starred restaurants. At night, rooftop bars and night markets keep the city’s energy alive

Recommended Trip Duration: 6 days

Best Time to Travel: Winter

Tourism Type: Cultural Gastronomic Adventure

Primary Language: Thai

Local Currency: Baht

Visa Required: Yes

Direct Flight from Seville: No

Approximate Trip Price per Person in Euros: 1500 €
