Processor building blocks

These are waveforms from the testbenches of my university processor design exercise, recorded with GHDL 4.1.0. Nothing here runs VHDL in your browser. The four blocks are separate and are not wired into a CPU.

Values at the cursor

More to the right: drag the cursor or use the arrow keys

Left and right arrows: previous or next change. Home and End: start and end.

Press or drag on the waveform to place the time cursor. The values at the cursor are listed too.

Each testbench runs one short sequence with 3 to 7 checks, so a pass shows those cases work and nothing more. The ROM holds five sample words from the exercise; the testbench reads three of them.

Times are simulated time in nanoseconds, taken from the GHDL runs. The source and testbench files are identified by SHA-256 in the recording manifest, together with the GHDL version and the container image used.
